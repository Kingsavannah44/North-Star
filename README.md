We are working with North Star Retail Co a mid size e-commerce company whose support team is overwhelmed by repetitive customer questions.
Our team is building a solution to help reduce the workload on the support team by addressing three common types of customer inquiries:
Order status questions
Return and refund questions
Stock availability questions
What It Covers

Our project covers the three main repetitive support areas identified by Northstar Retail Co:

Order Status
Helping customers understand where their orders are and how to check their order status.
Returns and Refunds
Providing information about Northstar's return and refund process, including what customers need to do and what they can expect.
Stock Availability
Helping customers determine whether a product is currently available and what options they have if an item is out of stock.
